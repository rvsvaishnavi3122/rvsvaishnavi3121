# AJIO Trust Layer

### Helping customers shop with confidence from discovery to purchase

---

# Executive Summary

Online fashion purchases involve a high level of uncertainty. While users can view ratings and reviews, they still struggle to answer questions like:

- Can I trust this seller?
- Will the product look the same in real life?
- Will it fit me?
- Am I likely to return it?

The AJIO Trust Layer introduces contextual trust signals throughout the shopping journey to help customers make more confident purchasing decisions while improving conversion and reducing returns.

---

# Problem

Fashion shopping relies heavily on visual confidence. Existing signals such as star ratings and written reviews often require users to spend time reading multiple reviews before making a decision.

This becomes even more challenging when shopping from newer sellers or products with limited purchase history.

As a result, users hesitate to purchase, abandon products, or return items because the product received doesn't match their expectations.

---

# User Research

To validate the problem, I conducted interviews with 12 frequent AJIO shoppers.

## Key Insights

- 10 out of 12 users said they hesitate to buy from unfamiliar sellers.
- Users spend additional time reading multiple reviews before making a purchase.
- Customer-uploaded photos are considered more trustworthy than catalog images.
- Users wanted more confidence around sizing, quality, and seller reliability than ratings alone provide.

### User Quote

> "If I don't know the seller and there aren't enough customer photos, I usually skip the product even if I like it."

---

# Opportunity

Current trust signals on AJIO are limited to ratings and reviews.

There is an opportunity to provide users with structured trust information before they make a purchase, reducing uncertainty without requiring them to read dozens of reviews.

This can especially help:

- First-time buyers
- Products from newer sellers
- High-return fashion categories

---

# Product Vision

Enable users to purchase fashion products with confidence by surfacing meaningful trust signals throughout the buying journey.

---

# Solution

Introduce an AI-powered Trust Layer across the shopping experience.

---

## 1. Product Discovery

Display a Trust Score directly on product listings.

Example:

⭐ Trust Score: 8.6 / 10

This helps users identify reliable products before opening the product page.

---

## 2. Product Details

Show a concise trust summary near the top of the Product Detail Page.

Example:

- Trusted Seller
- Low Return Rate
- Accurate Sizing
- Frequently Reordered

This allows users to quickly understand why the product is considered trustworthy.

---

## 3. Trust Breakdown

Users can expand the Trust Score to view additional insights.

Displayed information includes:

- Seller reliability
- Return rate compared with similar products
- Fit confidence
- Most common return reasons
- Quality consistency

---

## 4. Customer Reality Check

Surface customer-uploaded photos alongside catalog images.

Include comparisons such as:

- Catalog vs Customer Photo
- True Color
- Actual Fit

This helps users build realistic expectations before purchasing.

---

## 5. Purchase Guidance

Display contextual recommendations based on aggregated customer feedback.

Examples:

- Runs one size smaller
- Fabric feels lighter than expected
- Color appears slightly darker indoors

These insights reduce uncertainty before checkout.

---

## 6. Confidence Indicator

Before adding a product to the cart, reinforce confidence with a simple message.

Example:

✔ Most customers who purchased this item kept it after delivery.

---

# Feedback Loop

After delivery, users receive a lightweight feedback request.

Questions include:

- Was the size accurate?
- Did the product match the photos?
- Were you satisfied with the quality?

These responses continuously improve future Trust Scores.

# Risks & Mitigations

| Risk | Mitigation |
|-------|------------|
| Users ignore Trust Score | Test different placements and visual designs |
| Sellers question the scoring | Share transparent scoring methodology internally |
| Information overload | Start with four trust signals and expand based on user feedback |

---

# Why Existing Reviews Aren't Enough

| Existing Reviews | Trust Layer |
|------------------|------------|
| Unstructured feedback | Structured confidence signals |
| Users read multiple reviews | Information available instantly |
| Mixed opinions | Aggregated behavioral insights |
| Difficult to compare products | Standardized Trust Score |

# Expected Outcomes

- Improve product page conversion

# Reflection

This project reinforced the importance of reducing uncertainty rather than simply adding more information.

Instead of expecting users to read dozens of reviews, the Trust Layer summarizes the most relevant trust signals into a simple, understandable experience that helps users make faster and more confident purchase decisions.
