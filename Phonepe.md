# PhonePe Smart EMI - UPI-Native Affordability Prototype

## Problem
Research uncovered credit-accessibility gaps among underserved users on UPI rails — many are creditworthy in practice but invisible to traditional underwriting.

## Solution
Conceptualized a UPI-native EMI product using behavioral underwriting: transaction history and UPI usage patterns as signals of creditworthiness, rather than relying solely on traditional credit bureau data.

## Validation
Built and validated an MVP projecting:
- **+28%** checkout conversion
- **-22%** cart abandonment

## Why It Matters
UPI already captures rich behavioral signal on how users transact. Using that signal to underwrite affordability — rather than bolting on a generic BNPL product — extends credit access to users existing EMI products overlook, while keeping the experience native to how they already pay.
