# Product Strategy Analysis: Viz.ai

### Is Viz.ai solving a diagnostic problem or a care coordination problem?

---

# Executive Summary

Viz.ai is widely recognized for its AI-powered detection of time-sensitive diseases such as stroke, pulmonary embolism, and cardiovascular conditions.

However, after analyzing the product portfolio, I believe AI detection is not the end product—it is the entry point.

The real value lies in helping hospitals coordinate care faster after a diagnosis is made.

This case study explores whether Viz.ai should be positioned as a diagnostic AI product or as a clinical workflow platform.

---

# The Strategic Question

What business is Viz.ai really in?

Is it:

- An AI diagnostic tool?

or

- A clinical workflow platform that happens to use AI?

The answer influences positioning, product strategy, competitive advantage, and long-term growth.

---

# Background

In emergency medicine, every minute matters.

While detecting a condition quickly is important, diagnosis alone rarely changes patient outcomes.

The real challenge begins immediately after detection.

Hospitals must:

- Notify the right specialists
- Coordinate multiple care teams
- Transfer patients if required
- Begin treatment quickly

Any delay in this process directly affects patient outcomes.

---

# Job To Be Done

> When a patient presents with a time-sensitive condition, clinicians need to rapidly identify the disease, notify the right specialists, coordinate treatment decisions, and begin care as quickly as possible to improve patient outcomes.

The job is not simply detecting disease.

The job is helping patients receive treatment faster.

---

# Product Analysis

## Point Solution

A point solution focuses on a single problem.

### Scope

- AI image analysis
- Disease detection
- Clinical alert

Value is delivered only until diagnosis.

---

## Workflow Solution

A workflow solution extends beyond diagnosis.

It supports the complete clinical pathway.

Examples include:

- Automated specialist notification
- Secure communication
- Case collaboration
- Treatment coordination
- Patient transfer workflows
- Follow-up tracking

Value continues until treatment begins.

---

# Evidence

Viz.ai's product portfolio suggests expansion beyond diagnostic AI.

Products such as:

- Viz One
- Viz Assist

focus on communication and care coordination rather than image interpretation alone.

This indicates that diagnosis functions as the entry point into a broader clinical workflow platform.

---

# Strategic Comparison

| Dimension | Point Solution | Workflow Platform |
|-----------|----------------|------------------|
| Primary Value | Disease detection | Faster clinical decisions |
| Users | Radiologists | Entire care team |
| Sales Cycle | Simpler | More complex |
| Implementation | Faster | Requires integration |
| Customer Stickiness | Lower | Higher |
| Competitive Advantage | Easier to replicate | Stronger long-term moat |
| Expansion Opportunity | Limited | Multiple workflow extensions |

---

# Product Strategy

## Entry Strategy

Lead with AI-powered disease detection.

This creates an immediate, measurable value proposition that hospitals can evaluate quickly.

Examples:

- Faster stroke detection
- Reduced diagnosis time
- Improved clinical prioritization

---

## Expansion Strategy

Once adopted, expand into workflow products that support the broader care journey.

Potential expansion areas include:

- Care coordination
- Specialist collaboration
- Treatment workflows
- Transfer management
- Clinical communication

This increases product adoption across multiple hospital departments.

---

# Risks

| Risk | Mitigation |
|------|------------|
| Hospitals purchase only the diagnostic product | Bundle workflow capabilities after initial adoption |
| Longer implementation due to workflow integrations | Start with a focused clinical use case before expanding |
| Existing hospital systems create integration challenges | Prioritize interoperability with common EHR and communication platforms |

---

# Recommendation

Position Viz.ai as an AI diagnostic solution during customer acquisition.

Build the platform as a workflow solution internally.

Disease detection provides the initial value proposition, while workflow coordination creates long-term differentiation and customer retention.

In other words:

**Diagnosis wins the first sale.**

**Workflow wins long-term growth.**

---

# Reflection

This analysis reinforced an important product strategy principle:

Customers often purchase products because they solve one visible problem.

However, the strongest products expand beyond that initial use case and become embedded within the customer's workflow.

For Viz.ai, AI-powered diagnosis is the wedge.

Clinical workflow is the long-term competitive advantage.
